How an engagement starts

From first call to a written strategy in two weeks

  1. Consultation

    A 30-minute call with a partner. No cost, and we run a conflict check before we discuss details.

  2. Case assessment

    We review your documents and give you a candid read on strengths, risks and likely cost.

  3. Engagement letter

    Scope, staffing and fees in writing: hourly, flat-fee or contingency where appropriate.

  4. Strategy memo

    A written plan with milestones, a budget range and the decisions that are yours to make.
